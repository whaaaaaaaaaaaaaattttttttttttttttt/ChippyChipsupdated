Transparency

The Fine Print

We operate as a registered Minnesota Cottage Food Producer. Here's exactly what that means for you.

What you should know

  • These products are homemade and not subject to state inspection. They're made in a home kitchen, not an inspected commercial facility.
  • Minnesota only, in person. Under Minn. Stat. § 28A.152, homemade food can't be shipped or sent by courier. You pick up, or we personally deliver inside Minnesota. (State law changes to allow in-state mailing on August 1, 2027.)
  • Direct to you only. Our registration is not a food license and our chips cannot be resold by stores or restaurants.
  • Where we can sell: from our home, at a farmers' market, or at a community event — with us present.
  • Allergens: see each flavor's ingredient list. Our seasonings are made on shared equipment, so we can't guarantee an allergen-free bag.
  • Every bag is labeled with our name, registration number, the date it was made, full ingredients, and the homemade-food statement.
Allergen notice: artificial BBQ and sour cream & onion flavorings commonly contain milk and/or soy. Confirm the exact allergen breakdown with your seasoning supplier and print it on every bag label before you sell.
